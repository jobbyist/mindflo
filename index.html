The MindFlo approach

Not a quick fix. A space to find your footing.

Good support starts with being met where you are. MindFlo is built around thoughtful conversation, curiosity and the understanding that meaningful change takes time.

Meet Florence Kaunda.

MindFlo is led by Florence Kaunda, with a simple belief at its heart: people deserve a space where they can be heard, treated with respect and supported as they find their own way forward.

This is a place for honest conversations, thoughtful reflection and a more intentional relationship with yourself and the people around you.
